# Cleanup evidence checklist

For each exact candidate record the measured path and bytes, source observation and coverage, owning application/project, active-job/open-file evidence, regeneration command or verified recovery copy, retention obligation, proposed operation and action-time authorization. Unknown fields stay unknown. Path labels and timestamps are clues only.

- Dependencies: verify the project is inactive, the lockfile/toolchain exists and reinstall succeeds; prefer sharing/reusing environments before removing duplicates.
- Caches: verify the owning app documents an isolated regenerable cache. Use that app’s control; never treat all of Library or .hermes as cache.
- Logs: verify the writing process, retention obligations and closed archived files; fix rotation before removing approved archives.
- Downloads: verify original/recovery and select exact items. Do not classify Documents or synchronized originals as disposable.
- Models: verify active configuration, license/access and reproducible download. Preserve in-use weights.
- History, credentials, source, backups, databases, quarantine and recovery: preserve by default. Use dedicated retention/export/restore workflows.

Review a concrete plan before mutation. Revalidate identity and content as appropriate; skip changed or uncertain files. Prefer a supported recovery method. Trash movement does not establish free-space recovery; never empty Trash, remove snapshots, stop applications or delete recursively as a hidden follow-up. Record actual outcomes and remeasure on the same volume.
