# Japan bank and police handoff

Procedure observations dated 2026-10-09. Re-open each official landing page at
action time; fields, links, names and eligibility change. This workflow uses a
separately available authorized browser/email connector. It provides no direct
bank API or unattended submissions. Keep actual identities, accounts, device
serials, email tokens and receipts in the private vault.

## Recipient and authority

Distinguish victim, delegated representative and information provider. Collection
permission alone is not disclosure authority. Record existing delegation and the
selected recipient/purpose. Enter victim details only in victim fields;
representative details only in reporter/inputter fields. Clearly state the role.
If no representative route exists, verify the institution's procedure. Never
impersonate the victim, police, an exchange or counsel.

Group transfers by receiving bank, branch/account/holder; preserve dates, amounts,
source bank and evidence references. Distinguish accepted transfer requests from
executed settlement. Ask for investigation, necessary preservation/restriction,
prior relief decisions/payments and required representative papers. Historical
deadlines may have expired; ask about current status rather than asserting a new
application is eligible.

## Observed form workflows

**MUFG:** [Official relief page](https://www.bk.mufg.jp/info/hurikomesagi/index.html)
links to Web registration. Observed sequence: agreement, provisional email
registration, email link, explanation, inputter/transfer fields, confirmation,
final registration and completion email. Keep expiring email links private.
Provisional completion is not full registration. Verify current acceptance of
representative inputter details and explain the delegation in the narrative.
Capture all reviewed confirmation fields before final submission.

**DOCOMO SMTB Net Bank / former Sumishin SBI:** Start from
[the security page](https://www.netbk.co.jp/contents/security/malware/) and its
financial-crime inquiry link. Observed form: fraud category, reporter name,
notification email, recipient-specific transfer details, confirmation, submission
and completion. Match original receipt email and inquiry number to the report.
Brand changes do not establish a working submission API.

**PayPay:** [Official consultation page](https://www.paypay-bank.co.jp/security/contact/index.html)
leads to a form observed to require victim details. Do not put representative
contacts in victim fields. Missing victim details or an unclear representative
procedure leaves the draft pending for the official consultation route. An
information form may not answer questions or requests.

## Police and counsel

[NPA routes](https://www.npa.go.jp/bureau/cyber/soudan.html) provide current online
and jurisdiction-specific guidance. An online information report is distinct
from formal criminal complaint acceptance. If e-Gov requires a native client and
account, use the current official installation/login flow. Missing client,
credentials or blocked navigation is a blocker; do not silently install software,
bypass a browser security block or invent acceptance. Preserve conflicting prior
officer names as unresolved until matched to official evidence.

Provide counsel a factual chronology, originals, gross transfers, reconciled
returns, unresolved actor identity, jurisdiction and limitation/interim-relief
questions. Counsel determines remedies and formal process. Claimed Hong Kong or
Australia identities/locations remain claims until supported by evidence.

## Parameterized Japanese inquiry

Adapt after reviewing original receipts and reporter authority:

> 件名：詐欺被害に関する情報提供・調査及び被害回復手続の照会
>
> 私は［被害者］から銀行への資料提出を委任された［代理人］です。
> ［振込先銀行・支店・口座・名義］宛ての［日時・金額・振込元］について、
> ［証拠参照］に基づき情報提供します。確認された事実は［事実］、
> 未確認事項は［未確認事項］です。調査、必要な記録保全・取引制限の検討、
> 既存の被害回復申請・決定・支払状況及び代理人提出の必要書類をご案内ください。

A completion page alone cannot establish freezing, return or formal acceptance.
