# Alert triage

Establish the authorized account, time window and question from the task. Import supplied alert pages or explicitly collect the selected vendor read. Keep the receipt and original bytes.

Use cybersecurity_search for a literal indicator, vendor ID or known asset. Cite sourceSha256 and sourceRow for each observation; missing results mean absent in these retained samples only. Use cybersecurity_timeline and inspect unknownTimeCount before making sequencing claims. Preserve timezone uncertainty, clock skew and precision gaps.

Compare before/after only when the operator confirms the same tenant and collection scope. sampleAbsent is not resolution. Distinguish the vendor's detection from your hypothesis and list the evidence required to confirm it. This workflow does not isolate endpoints, remediate or launch collection on a device.
