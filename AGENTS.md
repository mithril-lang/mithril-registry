# Mithril Registry: agents and design boundaries

Existing executable artifacts remain governed by README.md, manifests and the deterministic build_index checks. Do not register a planned solution or a design contract as an installable Skill/MCP/Tool, and do not edit generated index/role briefs by hand.

## International investigation integration — Jun, 2026-10-08

The canonical non-executable contract is `solutions/international-investigation.json`; the design is `docs/security-integrations/international-investigation.md`. Fund consumes a commit/digest-pinned snapshot. Keep domestic authority and source custody, disclose only recipient/purpose/version-approved material, and distinguish dispatch, receipt, integrity, authority, completeness and judicial decisions.

Existing local signed evidence/custody/report evaluation components are building blocks, not cross-border transport or institution-qualified access. Separate the local stdio MCP from the Fund account-owned HTTP MCP. No official-channel connector, global case DB, identity federation, native case sync or automatic sharing is provided by this contract. Membership, case/material grants, jurisdiction review, translation provenance, external trust, uncertain outcomes and revocation require separate validation. Do not claim remote recall of exported bytes.

Police, prosecution, legal counsel, defense/counterparty and judges keep separate duties and access. AI drafts and translated summaries cannot replace original evidence or human authority/judicial decisions. Do not require private case data in public consultation, public Knowledge/Analysis/Kyber or source control. Follow `.cursor/rules/international-investigation.mdc` and preserve planned qualification in every derived record.
